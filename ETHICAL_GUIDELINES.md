# Ethical Use Guidelines for MBA Study Assistant

## Our Commitment to Academic Integrity

This study assistant is designed with a strong commitment to academic integrity, ethical use, and respect for intellectual property rights. We believe technology should enhance learning, not circumvent proper channels of education.

## Core Ethical Principles

### 1. **Legitimate Access Only**
This tool is designed to work EXCLUSIVELY with materials you have legitimate access to:
- ✅ Materials provided by your professors/instructors for course use
- ✅ Textbooks and resources you have legally purchased
- ✅ Your own class notes, recordings, and assignments
- ✅ Materials shared explicitly by instructors for study purposes
- ✅ Public domain educational resources
- ✅ Materials covered under fair use for educational purposes (within legal limits)

### 2. **No Circumvention of Restrictions**
You MUST NOT use this tool to:
- ❌ Bypass paywalls or subscription barriers
- ❌ Access materials behind login portals without authorization
- ❌ Copy or distribute copyrighted materials without permission
- ❌ Violate digital rights management (DRM) protections
- ❌ Access materials through unauthorized means

### 3. **Academic Integrity Enhancement, Not Replacement**
This tool is intended to:
- ✅ Help you understand and retain information better
- ✅ Identify areas where you need more study
- ✅ Create practice materials for self-assessment
- ✅ Organize and review your legitimate study materials
- ❌ NOT replace actual studying, reading, or engagement with course materials
- ❌ NOT generate content to submit as your own work
- ❌ NOT facilitate plagiarism or academic dishonesty

### 4. **Respect for Intellectual Property**
We respect the rights of:
- Authors and publishers of textbooks and academic materials
- Professors and instructors who create course content
- Educational institutions that develop curricula
- Creators of educational resources

## Specific Use Cases - What's Allowed

### ✅ PERMITTED Uses:
- Uploading lecture slides provided by your professor to create summary notes
- Processing textbook chapters you've purchased to generate study questions
- Converting your own handwritten notes (via image upload) to searchable text
- Creating practice quizzes from study guides distributed by your instructor
- Summarizing research papers you have legal access to for literature review
- Processing case studies provided in your MBA curriculum

### ❌ PROHIBITED Uses:
- Uploading pirated textbooks or course materials
- Processing materials from subscription services without personal access
- Sharing generated questions/notes from copyrighted materials with others who don't have access
- Using the tool to avoid purchasing required course materials
- Attempting to access restricted university portals or systems

## How to Approach Your Professor for Study Materials

### Step-by-Step Guide:

#### 1. **Prepare Your Request**
- Identify specific materials that would help your study (slides, notes, etc.)
- Explain how processing them with study aids will enhance your learning
- Emphasize that you want to better understand and engage with the material
- Have a clear, respectful request ready

#### 2. **Choose the Right Time and Method**
- Request during office hours or after class when they're not rushed
- Consider email if that's their preferred communication method
- Be prepared to explain what the tool does and doesn't do

#### 3. **Frame the Request Around Learning Enhancement**
Sample script:
> "Professor [Name], I'm working to better understand the concepts from [specific topic/lecture] and was wondering if I could have access to [specific material: slides/notes/etc.] in a format that would allow me to use study aids to help me process and retain the information more effectively. I want to make sure I'm fully grasping the material you're teaching, and having the ability to create summary notes and practice questions from the authorized materials would help me identify areas where I need to focus more. I assure you I will use any materials strictly for my personal study purposes in accordance with university policies."

#### 4. **Address Potential Concerns Proactively**
Be ready to discuss:
- How you'll keep materials confidential and not share them
- That you understand and will follow university academic integrity policies
- That the tool is for personal study enhancement only
- That you will not redistribute or misuse any materials
- That you're happy to show them how the tool works if they're interested

#### 5. **Provide Alternatives if Needed**
If they're uncomfortable sharing editable formats, ask if they can provide:
- Printed versions you could scan (with their permission)
- Specific sections they'd recommend focusing on
- Suggested study guides or resources they approve of
- Practice problems or questions they've created

#### 6. **Follow Up Appropriately**
- Thank them for their time regardless of the outcome
- If granted access, use the materials exactly as discussed
- Consider sharing anonymized, aggregated feedback on how the study aids helped (if appropriate)
- Respect any boundaries they set

## Best Practices for Ethical Study Enhancement

### **Before Using Any Study Tool:**
1. Verify you have legitimate access to the source material
2. Review your institution's academic integrity policy
3. Consider if the tool enhances rather than replaces learning
4. Be prepared to explain how you're using the tool if asked

### **During Use:**
1. Keep materials secure and private
2. Do not share processed copyrighted materials with others
3. Use generated questions for self-assessment only
4. Continue to engage with original materials deeply
5. Verify AI-generated content for accuracy when possible

### **After Use:**
1. Delete temporary files if they contain sensitive information
2. Consider what you actually learned vs. what the tool generated
3. Apply knowledge to real-world scenarios and discussions
4. Seek clarification from professors on concepts you found challenging

## Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious of tools or services that:
- Promise to "get you answers" without doing the work
- Advertise ways to access restricted or paid content for free
- Encourage sharing of copyrighted materials
- Have unclear terms of service regarding content usage
- Don't mention academic integrity or ethical use
- Seem too good to be true for exam preparation

## Remember: The Goal is Learning

The ultimate goal of any study aid should be to:
- Deepen your understanding of the subject matter
- Help you identify knowledge gaps
- Prepare you to apply concepts in real-world scenarios
- Support your journey to becoming a competent business leader
- Respect the educational process and intellectual property rights

Technology is most powerful when it enhances, rather than replaces, the learning experience. Use this tool as a supplement to, not a substitute for, engaging authentically with your MBA education.
